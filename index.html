About the Studio

Light doesn't wait.
Neither do we.

The Grandmoon Studio was founded by Rajdeep Bhatri, who has been shooting weddings for 6 years and has covered 100+ weddings across the country. What started as wedding photography has grown into a full-service studio for every kind of celebration.

From candid wedding photography and cinematic films to drone coverage, album designing, video mixing, and podcast shoots — we handle the entire story, start to finish, in-house.

What We Do

Our Services

Wedding Photography

We capture every emotion, tradition and beautiful moment of your wedding day with natural and cinematic photography. From candid moments to important family memories, every frame is carefully captured.

Cinematic Films

Your wedding story deserves to feel like a film. We create cinematic wedding films with emotional storytelling, beautiful visuals, music and carefully edited sequences.

Drone Coverage

Capture your special moments from a completely different perspective with professional aerial photography and cinematic drone coverage.

Album Designing

We design premium wedding albums that preserve your memories beautifully. Every page is designed with attention to photographs, emotions and storytelling.

Video Editing

From wedding highlights to complete event films, our editing process combines music, storytelling, colour grading and cinematic transitions.

Podcast Shoots

Professional multi-camera podcast shoots with clean visuals, proper framing and high-quality video production for creators and businesses.
